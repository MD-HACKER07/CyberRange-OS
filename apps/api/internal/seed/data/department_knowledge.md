## About this platform (CyberRange OS)
Self-hosted red-team/blue-team cyber range with the on-premise CyberSec AI model.
Red Team: scan an isolated sandbox, pick a target, launch real attacks with an AI pentest copilot.
Blue Team: a live SOC console shows alerts in real time; triage with an AI SOC copilot (MTTD/MTTR tracked).
Everything runs on the department server; no student data leaves the institution.
Log in with your college email or roll number; reports and scores are in the Reports section.
